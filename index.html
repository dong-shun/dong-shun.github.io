Research

Job Market Paper

Accounting for Industry Heterogeneity in Predicting Corporate Default Probability: A Semiparametric Learning Approach

with Shaobo Li, Ben Sherwood, and Xiaorui Zhu

Revise and Resubmit, European Journal of Operational Research

We develop a semiparametric learning approach for corporate default prediction that combines a common financial risk index with industry-specific flexible link functions. The model captures nonlinear industry heterogeneity while retaining an interpretable low-dimensional risk index, and delivers improved predictive and economic performance in a large sample of U.S. publicly traded firms.
